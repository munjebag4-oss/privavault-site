PrivaVault Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

PrivaVault ("the app") is built by KJ Smart App Lab. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Summary

PrivaVault does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data. Your photos and videos never leave your device.

Your photos and videos

  • Files you add to PrivaVault are encrypted with AES-256-GCM and stored only in the app's private storage on your device.
  • We do not operate any server that receives your files. There is no account, no cloud sync and no backup service.
  • Your encryption keys are protected by your PIN and by a secret kept in your device's secure hardware (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore). We cannot access, view, or recover your files or your PIN.

Permissions

  • Photos / media library: used only to import the files you choose, to delete the originals when you ask, and to save files you choose to unhide.
  • Camera: used only when you take a photo inside the app. The photo goes directly into your encrypted vault.
  • Face ID / fingerprint: optional, used only to unlock the app. Biometric data is handled by your operating system and is never accessible to us.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We do not receive your payment details. The app checks with the store whether you own PrivaVault Pro.

Analytics and advertising

The app contains no analytics, no advertising SDKs and no tracking.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Data deletion

All data is stored on your device. Deleting a vault in the app or uninstalling the app permanently erases your encrypted files. Because we hold no copies, deleted data cannot be recovered.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.
