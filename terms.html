The service

PrivaVault encrypts photos and videos you choose and stores them only on your device. There is no account and no server, so we cannot see, back up, or restore your files.

Your responsibility

  • Remember your PIN. Because only you hold it, a forgotten PIN cannot be reset and the files in that vault cannot be recovered.

  • Before using "delete originals", open the item inside PrivaVault and confirm it displays correctly.
